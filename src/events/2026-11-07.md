My work is included in this exhibition in Providence, curated by friend and fellow artist {% extLink "Isabella Uliasz", "https://isabellauliasz.com/" %}.

The opening is from 5-7pm on Saturday, November 7, and the show will be on view until November 28. The work ranges from sculpture and glass to ephemeral light art to multimedia works.

Vic Xu • Rachel Efruss • Samantha Grabelle • Zach Storm • George Stadnik • Laura Perdrizet • Kelly Clare • Paloma Kop & Andrei Jay • Dot Bergen • Adda Farcus • Enid Corcoran • Brandon Barr • Isabella Uliasz • Lee Casstevens • Erin Taylor • Anya Detonnancourt
