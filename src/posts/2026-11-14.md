Last Saturday, we attended the opening of *Adjusting to the Dark*, a group exhibition in Providence, RI curated by fellow artist {% extLink "Isabella Uliasz", "https://isabellauliasz.com/" %}. Our work was installed alongside a variety of multimedia works, including prints, sculptures, and other video works. While the media was diverse, thematic relationships of light/dark, positive/negative space, and color/monochrome appeared throughout the gallery space.

Curator's statement:

> There is a latent space between remembering and experiencing something new. The brain receives an input and does its best to approximate, comparing it against what is known. In our memories, we find smells and strange emotions that may not be attached to a specific moment, but stand quietly in the vast bank of the familiar, insisting You Know Me. The senses give shape to experience, and experiences shape the mind such that it can alter the senses. This mutual forming, and the line separating sensory observation and narrative creation, are the focus of this exhibition. *Adjusting to the Dark* places a bridge in the void, suggesting time, place, and gesture as remedies for unanswerable questions. It honors the autumnal themes of decay and increasing darkness, and centers strategic application of life-giving light.

The exhibition is on view through November 28. Many thanks to Izzy for all the work that went into organizing, curating, and installing this exhibition. We enjoyed the excuse to visit Providence, and hope to spend more time there in the future.
